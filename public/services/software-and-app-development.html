Software and Mobile App Development in Nepal

Training is one way we help. The other is building the tool itself. Through our SAFAL AI team we design and develop custom software, mobile apps and AI automations for organisations in Nepal and abroad.

NATDS Services · Nov 2026

What we build

  • Internal web software — dashboards, document workflows, reporting tools and portals.
  • Mobile apps — practical tools for field teams, students, teachers or customers.
  • AI automations — document parsing, draft generation, routing and review workflows.
  • Prototypes — a small working version to test before committing to a full build.

We start from a real workflow, not a technology trend. If a spreadsheet and a clear process solve the problem, we will say so.

How a build runs

  1. Discovery. We map the problem, the users and the data involved.
  2. Scope. A written agreement covers features, milestones, access and handover.
  3. Prototype. A small working version lets you test the idea early.
  4. Build. Development proceeds in agreed stages with regular check-ins.
  5. Handover. Your team receives the product, documentation and training to run it.

Training and product work together

Most engagements begin with AI training. Teams map a workflow in the room; if it is worth turning into a product, we scope that separately. This keeps the build grounded in real work and gives your team the skills to use it.
