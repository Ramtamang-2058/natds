AI Training and Software Across Nepal

NATDS is based in Kathmandu, but delivery is not limited to the valley. We train teams live online across all 77 districts, visit major cities for on-site workshops, and build custom software and mobile products remotely.

NATDS Coverage · Nov 2026

How delivery works

Live online everywhere. The same eight-session curriculum reaches teams in every district of Nepal. Participants join from their own office, work on non-confidential examples, and leave with a 30-day plan.

On-site in the Kathmandu Valley. We regularly deliver in Kathmandu, Lalitpur and Bhaktapur, including work with IME Group and Prakriti Resource Center.

On-site in other cities by arrangement. We can plan visits for Pokhara, Biratnagar, Dharan, Itahari, Butwal, Mahendranagar, Nepalgunj, Dhangadhi and other cities when a team needs in-person sessions. Share group size, dates and preferred format on WhatsApp.

Product work is remote by default. Software and mobile app development is scoped and delivered with the SAFAL AI team, with check-ins that fit your time zone.
