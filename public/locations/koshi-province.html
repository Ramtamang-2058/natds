AI Training in Biratnagar, Dharan and Koshi Province

Koshi teams deal with the same reports, documents and decisions as Kathmandu teams. The difference is delivery: on-site where it helps, live online where it saves time.

NATDS Coverage · Nov 2026

Biratnagar, Dharan and Itahari

We can arrange on-site AI training for teams in Biratnagar, Dharan and Itahari. Participants work on non-confidential examples from their own roles, build one workflow together, and leave with a 30-day plan owned by their team.

For groups spread across Koshi, live online sessions follow the same curriculum. A manager in Biratnagar, an operations lead in Dharan and a researcher in Itahari can learn the same method without travel.

All 14 districts, online

Live online delivery reaches Bhojpur, Dhankuta, Ilam, Jhapa, Khotang, Morang, Okhaldhunga, Panchthar, Sankhuwasabha, Solukhumbu, Sunsari, Taplejung, Terhathum and Udayapur. On-site visits outside the main cities can be discussed when you enquire.

What we can build after training

If the workflow you map in training deserves to become a real tool, NATDS and the SAFAL AI team can build it. That includes internal web software, mobile apps and AI automations — not just slides and certificates. See software and mobile app development.
